# FOCUS – One Goal, One Thing

FOCUS is a deliberately minimal focus app.

It is not a productivity tool.
It does not help you do more.

It helps you **stop avoiding what matters**.

---

## Philosophy

FOCUS is built on a simple belief:

> Clarity comes from commitment, not options.

Most tools fail because they add features to reduce discomfort.
FOCUS does the opposite.

It removes choice.
It removes noise.
It removes excuses.

---

## Core Rules

FOCUS enforces a few non‑negotiable rules:

* You can have **one goal only**
* You can choose **one meaningful task per day**
* You focus deeply, without multitasking
* You reflect honestly, without justification

There are no streaks.
There are no badges.
There is no motivation layer.

Only truth.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Set One Goal

You choose a single goal that matters *right now*.

Not forever.
Not your life purpose.
Just the thing you are committing to.

Once set, the goal is intentionally hard to change.

> Commitment should feel slightly uncomfortable.

---

### 2. Choose Today’s One Thing

Each day, you choose **one task** that moves you closer to your goal.

Not a list.
Not a plan.

Just one thing worth your attention.

You also choose how much time you will give it.

---

### 3. Focus

When focus starts:

* The screen goes quiet
* The goal is subtly visible
* Time is the only thing moving

Ending a session early is allowed.
But it is not encouraged.

FOCUS will remind you:

> Ending early won’t bring you closer to your goal.

---

### 4. Reflect

After each session, you must answer one question:

**How did it go?**

* Progress
* Neutral
* Failed

You may add one sentence of reflection.
Or none.

There is no analysis.
No advice.

Just acknowledgment.

---

### 5. Weekly Truth

Once a week, FOCUS shows you reality.

Not averages.
Not charts.

Just:

* Days you showed up
* Days you didn’t

And a short sentence describing what that pattern means.

Sometimes the truth is encouraging.
Sometimes it is uncomfortable.

FOCUS does not soften it.

---

## What FOCUS Is Not

FOCUS intentionally avoids:

* Task managers
* Productivity dashboards
* Habit trackers
* Notifications designed to motivate
* Gamification or rewards

If you are looking for encouragement, this may not be the right tool.

---

## Platform Notes

FOCUS is currently built as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**.

* Best experienced on mobile
* Notifications use system default sounds
* No custom audio or aggressive reminders

Desktop is supported for:

* Goal setting
* Reflection
* Weekly review

Mobile is preferred for:

* Focus sessions

This is intentional.

---

## Design Principles

* Fewer decisions beat better decisions
* Silence is part of the experience
* Discomfort is a signal, not a bug
* Software should not lie to make you feel better

---

## Who This Is For

FOCUS is for people who:

* Are capable but scattered
* Are busy but not progressing
* Want clarity more than motivation

If you want a tool that pushes you gently,
FOCUS will feel too quiet.

If you want a tool that tells the truth,
FOCUS may stay with you.
