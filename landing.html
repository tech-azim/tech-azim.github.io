Focus over optimization

FOCUS does one thing: it holds you to your word.

Set one goal. Not five. One.

Choose one meaningful task each day. Not a list. One thing.

Do it. Reflect honestly.

The app doesn't celebrate effort.

It doesn't reward you for showing up.

It tracks commitment, not activity.

At the end of each week, you see the truth.

How many days you actually focused.

How many days you were just busy.

How it works

1. Set your goal

What do you want? Say it clearly. This doesn't change unless you start over.

2. Choose your one thing

Each day, pick the task that matters. You get one session. That's it.

3. Work in focus

Start the timer. The app stays out of your way. If you end early, it asks why.

4. Reflect honestly

Did you make progress? The app saves your answer. One entry per day.

Weekly Truth

Every week, you see how many days you focused.

Not how many tasks you completed.

Not how many hours you logged.

Days. That's all.

The app responds with one sentence.

Sometimes encouraging. Sometimes not.

"Two focused days in seven. You are busy, not committed."

If the truth is uncomfortable, that's the point.
